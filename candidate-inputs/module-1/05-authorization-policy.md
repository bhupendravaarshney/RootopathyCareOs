# Module 1 authorization policy candidate

**Artifact kind:** `authorization-policy`  
**Status:** `CANDIDATE_FOR_APPROVAL`  
**Candidate version:** `m1-candidate-1`  
**Approval:** Not granted

## Decision baseline

Authorization is deny-by-default and evaluated from current database membership, scope, registry version, operation, subject, organization lifecycle, assurance, and delegation inside the tenant transaction. Browser organization selection and hidden actions are never evidence. Unknown, disabled, retired, or version-mismatched roles/permissions/operations deny.

### Roles and delegation

| Role                         | Candidate purpose and grant boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Delegation                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `organization_owner`         | Final-owner role; all M1 permissions except it remains subject to maker-checker, self-target, purpose, and scope rules. At least one indefinite active owner must remain.                                                                                                   | May assign any non-owner role and initiate owner transfer. | `organization_administrator` | Day-to-day profile, contact, network, service, scheme-draft, invitation, membership-read, readiness, history-read operations. | `configuration_editor`       | Create/update draft organization/network/service/scheme configuration, run validation, and submit. No approval/activation.                                                                                                                                                  | None.                                                                                               |
| `configuration_approver`     | Read submitted configuration/evidence, approve/reject, and activate if not maker. Cannot edit the candidate being decided.                                                                                                                                                  | None.                                                                                               |
| `security_administrator`     | Manage invitation/access requests and initiate MFA reset. Final execution still follows owner/maker-checker rules.                                                                                                                                                          | May assign viewer only; cannot grant itself or another security role.                               |
| `auditor`                    | Read minimum-necessary configuration history and audit detail after recent authentication. No mutations or exports by default.                                                                                                                                              | None.                                                                                               |
| `export_approver`            | Independently authorize restricted history/audit exports and access approval evidence. Cannot request/approve the same restricted export.                                                                                                                                   | None.                                                                                               |

Roles are organization scoped in candidate 1. Facility-scoped grants may narrow `configuration_editor` and viewer access, but cannot broaden a permission and do not apply to owners, approval, audit, exports, schemes, or organization-wide settings. `local_bootstrap` and every reference registry remain ineligible for production.

### Operation policy

| Operation group                                                           | Denial                                               | Assurance and controls                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active configuration reads and readiness                                  | Hidden `404`                                         | Current membership and read permission; no RA. Minimum-necessary fields.                                                            |
| Draft/list/detail reads                                                   | Hidden `404`                                         | Matching manage/read permission and scope. Restricted reason/evidence fields are separately projected.                              |
| Draft create/update, hours batch, assignment                              | Explicit `403` after organization access is known    | Reason, strong ETag where updating, idempotency, exact scope, governed evidence. No RA unless changing security-sensitive settings. |
| Identifier verification, governance contact, lifecycle suspension/closure | Explicit `403`                                       | MFA, RA <= 10 minutes, reason, impact checks. Closure and owner/governance gaps may require MC through configuration approval.      |
| Invitation issue/revoke                                                   | Hidden for target membership; explicit action denial | MFA, RA <= 10 minutes, reason, delegable-role ceiling, no owner invitation.                                                         |
| Membership role/scope change or revoke                                    | Hidden target, explicit action denial                | MFA, RA <= 5 minutes, reason, MC, target separation, final-owner database guard.                                                    |
| Administrative MFA reset                                                  | Hidden target                                        | MFA, RA <= 5 minutes, reason, maker/checker/target all distinct where two owners exist; approved maker performs execution.          |
| Validation and submit                                                     | Explicit                                             | Reason on submit, exact candidate/revision/result, freshness <= 15 minutes, idempotency. Maker recorded.                            |
| Approve/reject and activate                                               | Explicit                                             | MFA, RA <= 5 minutes, reason, checker distinct from maker; approval <= 30 minutes; activator not maker; exact digest/revision.      |
| Audit detail                                                              | Hidden                                               | MFA and RA <= 10 minutes; purpose recorded. Access to restricted detail is itself audited.                                          |
| Export request/access                                                     | Hidden                                               | Purpose/legal basis, reason, bounded projection, MFA and RA <= 5 minutes; access grant <= 10 minutes. Restricted export uses MC.    |

Organization MFA enforcement is mandatory for owners, administrators, configuration approvers, security administrators, auditors, and export approvers. Those roles cannot self-disable MFA while the membership remains active. A user may replace factors/recovery codes after RA; loss uses the governed administrative reset. Viewer/editor MFA may be made mandatory by organization policy, but enabling enforcement cannot strand the final owner and requires a readiness preview.

### Service identities and emergency access

- `m1-scheduled-transition-v1`, `m1-export-worker-v1`, and `m1-outbox-publisher-v1` are separate non-interactive identities with exact operation grants, tenant discovery disabled, short-lived managed credentials, 90-day maximum rotation, and no browser/session role.
- Service identities cannot approve, change policy, discover tenants, or execute work without a tenant-bound authorized record. Their database transaction context includes identity, purpose, operation, tenant, and source evidence.
- Candidate 1 defines no general break-glass role. Emergency organization suspension is a separately deployed incident operation requiring two security authorities, MFA/RA, incident ID, maximum one-hour grant, immutable evidence, and post-incident review. It cannot bypass tenant isolation, final-owner integrity, schema validation, or evidence append-only rules.

## Verification and acceptance

- Migration-owned registries contain exact roles, permissions, grants, delegation edges, operations, policy version, and disabled-by-default production activation flag.
- Tests attack wildcard grants, role-name inference, stale membership, wrong scope, hidden-resource disclosure, self-approval, final-owner removal, delegation escalation, retired policy, service-identity crossover, and direct SQL.
- Every M1-01 through M1-23 control maps to one operation; permission-hidden actions and explicit denials match the policy table without client-side authority.

## Approval boundary

The grant matrix and assurance rules are a candidate replacement for the provisional registry. Production preflight must reject this registry until its exact digest and activation decision are owner-approved; candidate validation never activates it.
